---
title: "Invitations"
description: "Invite people to a workspace and respond to invitations."
---

An invitation is an offer, sent to an email address, to join a workspace as an editor or a viewer. Owners send invitations, and the person invited chooses to accept or decline.

## Invite a member

Minimum role: owner.

1. Open **Members** in the sidebar.
2. Select **Invite member**.
3. Enter the **Email address** of the person.
4. Choose a **Role**, either **Viewer** or **Editor**.
5. Select **Send invitation**.

SnippetVault emails the person a link to the invitation. Owner is not offered, because a workspace has exactly one owner. See [Members and roles](/members-and-roles) for what each role can do.

An invitation can be sent only if:

- the email address does not already belong to a member of the workspace;
- there is no active invitation for that email address in the workspace.

An invitation is bound to the email address it was sent to. Only an account with that email address can accept it.

## Accept or decline an invitation

Minimum role: none. You need a signed-in account with the invited email address.

You can respond in two places.

### From your invitations

1. Sign in and open **Invitations** in the sidebar.
2. Find the invitation. It shows the workspace, the role, who invited you and when it expires.
3. Select **Accept** or **Decline**.

When you accept, you join the workspace with the role in the invitation, and it appears in your workspace menu. When you decline, the invitation is closed and you do not join.

### From the email link

1. Open the link in the email.
2. Check the workspace, the person who invited you and the role.
3. Select **Accept invitation**.

You then land on your dashboard as a member of the workspace. The link page offers only accepting. To decline, use **Invitations** in the sidebar.

## If you are not signed in

The invitation page asks you to sign in or create an account with the invited email address.

- **Sign in** returns you to the invitation after you log in.
- **Create account** takes you to registration. After you have registered, open the invitation link again.

If you are signed in with a different email address, the page says which address the invitation was sent to and offers to sign you out, so you can use the right account.

## Expiry and invalid invitations

An invitation is valid for 24 hours after it is sent. The invitation page shows an unavailable message when the link:

- is not a valid invitation link;
- has expired;
- was already accepted or declined.

Ask the workspace owner to send a new invitation.

Other reasons an invitation cannot be accepted:

- You are signed in with a different email address than the one invited. Invitations match by email address, so the page asks you to sign out and use the invited account, and the **Invitations** inbox of another account does not list it.
- You are already a member of the workspace.
